Your time. Your mission.

Turn your Pebble into a classic spy-watch display. PebbleEye pairs a bold,
segmented bezel with a crisp clock and eight coordinated colour palettes:
Classic, Amber, Ice, Orchid, Lagoon, Ember, Silver and Rose.

Choose Digital for an instant read, Analogue for a traditional dial, or Hybrid
for full-size hands with digital time written over the dial.

Make it yours:
• Three display modes and eight colour palettes.
• Smooth, readable time digits with crisp date and status labels.
• Five date formats, plus your watch’s 12-hour or 24-hour setting.
• Optional battery and phone connection information.
• Optional seconds hand while the backlight is on.
• Time 2 only: optional 12-second theme on a double tap of the watch.
• Time 2 and Round 2: optional three-second vibration rhythm on a double tap.
• A settings preview, easy Undo and clear Save controls.

Designed specifically for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2. Seconds are off by
default; regular timekeeping updates once a minute. Music is also off by default
and respects mute and Quiet Time. Vibration is off by default and respects Quiet
Time. Choose music or vibration on Time 2; both cannot be enabled together.
Round 2 has no speaker. Wake the backlight, hold your wrist still, then double-tap
gently within eight seconds. Wake the light again to repeat. No account required.
