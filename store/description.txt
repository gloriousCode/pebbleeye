Your time. Your mission.

Turn your Pebble into a classic spy-watch display. PebbleEye pairs a bold,
segmented bezel with a crisp clock and three coordinated colour palettes:
mission green, warm amber and cool ice.

Choose Digital for an instant read, Analogue for a traditional dial, or Hybrid
for full-size hands with digital time written over the dial.

Make it yours:
• Three display modes and three colour palettes.
• Five date formats, plus your watch’s 12-hour or 24-hour setting.
• Optional battery and phone connection information.
• Optional seconds hand while the backlight is on.
• A settings preview, easy Undo and clear Save controls.

Designed specifically for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2. Seconds are off by
default; regular timekeeping updates once a minute. No account required.

An independent, unofficial homage to classic spy-game watch displays.
