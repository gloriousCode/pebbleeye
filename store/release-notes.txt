PebbleEye is now a standalone watchface for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.

Choose digital, analogue or full-size hybrid time, with eight coordinated colour
palettes. Native time-digit smoothing improves readability while preserving
measured screen bounds. Customise the date, watch status and optional seconds hand
from a clearer settings page with preview, Undo and reliable settings delivery.

This release removes the unused word-clock implementation, corrects configuration
hosting and responds immediately when the backlight changes. Seconds remain off
by default.

Time 2 adds optional double-tap theme playback, disabled by default and respecting
mute and Quiet Time. Round 2 has no speaker and does not include the audio.
