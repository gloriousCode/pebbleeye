PebbleEye 1.6.3

Gentle double taps now use sampled acceleration during a short backlight window.
Wake the light, hold your wrist still and tap twice within eight seconds.
Detection stops after one gesture, when the light goes out, or after eight seconds.

Time 2 now supports the three-second vibration rhythm as well as music. Choose
one mode or leave both off; the settings page and watch prevent both running
together. Existing conflicting preferences migrate to vibration. Round 2 retains
vibration and omits the audio resource. Mute and Quiet Time are respected.

Native builds and synthetic gesture/lifecycle tests pass. Physical sensitivity
and battery impact still need measurement on watches. Store images are unchanged
because this release changes interaction rather than the rendered face.
